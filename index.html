02 / FPGA audio

Lyrebird

A sixteen-voice polyphonic synthesizer on an AMD Kria KV260. A USB MIDI controller is read on the Linux side and written into registers in the programmable logic, where an HLS DSP core generates 24-bit, 48 kHz audio: phase-modulation voices with wavefolding, filters, envelopes and LFOs, and a shared reverb, played out over I2S.

Boarbarktree.

My maths YouTube channel. Currently inactive, but it may well come back.

The channel

Hand-drawn explainer videos on algebraic topology, including a three-part series, You Could Have Invented Homology. Each video was an enormous amount of hand animation in Adobe Animate.

Where it's at

It went quiet when I started my PhD, because I couldn't keep up that pace alongside it. I still want to make the videos, which is why I'm building Whiteboard: a way to make them without animating everything by hand.
