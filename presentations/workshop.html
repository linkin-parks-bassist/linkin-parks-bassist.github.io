Linux receives standard MIDI, allocates voices, and publishes compact state. PL adopts a coherent snapshot through shadow registers and a commit, invokes HLS once per sample, then crosses completed PCM into the I2S clock domain. The HLS algorithm itself knows nothing about MIDI, AXI or I2S.

AMD On Wheels NZ · together we advance
This was the earlier, smaller accumulation core, not the complete Lyrebird. Baseline: 114 cycles, 13.301 ns. The agent replaced a divider with a reciprocal multiply: 111 cycles, 1,588 fewer LUTs, and exactly the same 13.301 ns, because the real critical path was the serial sixteen-voice floating-point sum. That experiment was discarded. Minimising cycles is the default instinct; the spec said we had 2,000.
